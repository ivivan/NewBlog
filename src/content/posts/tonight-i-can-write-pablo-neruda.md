<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">Tonight I can write the saddest lines.
Write, for example, ‘The night is shattered
and the blue stars shiver in the distance.'</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">The night wind revolves in the sky and sings.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">Tonight I can write the saddest lines.
I loved her. and sometimes she loved me too.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">Through nights like thy one I held her in my arms.
I kissed her again and again under the endless sky.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">She loved me, sometimes I loved her too.
How could one not have loved her great still eyes.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">Tonight I can write the saddest lines.
To think that I do not have her. To feel that I have lost her.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">To hear the immense night, still more immense without her.
And the verse falls to the soul like dew to the pasture. W</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">What does it matter that my love could not keep her.
The night is shattered and she is not with me.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">This is all. In the distance someone is singing. In the distance.
My soul is not satisfied that it has lost her.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">My sight searches for her as though to go to her.
My heart looks for her, and she is not with me.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">She same night whitening the same trees.
We, of that time, are no longer the same.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">I no longer love her, that's certain, but how I loved her.
My voice tried to find the wind to touch her hearing,</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">Another's. She will be another's. Like my kisses before.
Her voice. Her bright body. Her infinite eyes.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">I no longer love her, that's certain, but maybe I love her.
Love is so short, forgetting is so long.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">Because through nights like this one I held her in my arms
my soul is not satisfied that it has lost her.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">Though this be the last pain that she makes me suffer
and these the last verses that I write for her.</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;font-size:small;">今夜我可以写</span>

<span style="font-family:微软雅黑;">
</span><span style="font-size:small;"> 今夜我可以写下最哀伤的诗句。
写，譬如，“夜镶满群星，
而星星遥远地发出蓝光并且颤抖“。
夜风在天空中回旋并低唱。
今夜我可以写下最哀伤的诗句。
我爱她，而且有时她也爱我。
如同今晚的夜，我曾拥她入怀，
在无尽的天空下一遍又一遍地吻她。
她爱我，有时我也爱她。
怎会不爱上她那沉静的双眼?
今夜我可以写下最哀伤的诗句。
去想我并不拥用她，感觉我已失去她。
去聆听广阔的夜，因没有她而更加广阔。
诗句坠入我的灵魂，如同露水落在牧草上。
我的爱若不能拥有她又会怎样？
群星满夜
而她不在我身边。
这就是一切了。
远处有人在唱着歌。
那么远。
我的空虚因为没有她。
我的目光搜寻她，想要把她拉近；
我的心寻找她
而她并没有和我在一起。
相同的夜让相同的树木泛白。
彼时，我们也不再相似如初。
我不再爱她，这是确定的，但我曾多爱她。
我的声音试着循风来抚摸她的听觉。
别人的
如同她曾接受的我的千吻，
她的声音，她洁白的身体，她无尽的双眼，
她将会是别人的。
我不再爱她，这是确定的，但也许我还爱她。
爱情太短，遗忘太长。
借着如同今晚的夜，我曾拥她入怀
我的灵魂因失去了她而失落。
这是她最后一次让我承受的伤痛
而这些，便是我为她而写的最后的诗句。</span>
